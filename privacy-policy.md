# Privacy Policy for Hold or Stop

Hold or Stop does not collect personal information, browsing history, website content, location data, or advertising identifiers. The game runs within the extension and does not include advertising, analytics, or user tracking. Game data is not sent to a server, sold, or shared with third parties.

## Data Stored

The extension stores the selected language, sound setting, reduced-motion preference, best score, best precision, best precision streak, and number of games played. This information is stored locally and is used only to display the user's settings and records.

## Why the `storage` Permission Is Required

The `storage` permission is required only to keep game settings and records after the extension popup is closed. It is not used to access open pages, browsing history, device files, or account information, and it does not send data outside the browser.

## External Link

The donation link opens an external website only when selected by the user. The privacy policy of that website applies after the user leaves the extension.
